// the problem

Your agent is a stranger wearing a helpful mask.

It knows your calendar. Your to-do list. Your location. But it doesn't know what keeps you up at night, what you're avoiding, or whether you're actually happy. The understanding score measures the gap between 'useful' and 'known'.
